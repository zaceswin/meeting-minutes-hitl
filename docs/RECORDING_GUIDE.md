# Recording guide: fix the audio before you fix the model

Most failures in a noisy hall happen before any software runs. Procedure beats ML here.

1. **Phone in the middle, face up, on a soft surface** (folded cloth), not in a pocket, not on a metal table. Airplane mode on so calls don't interrupt.
2. **Use the phone's voice-memo app** and record m4a/wav. Don't use speakerphone or screen-record.
3. **One speaker at a time.** The chair says: "Please wait for the phone / wait to be named." Cross-talk is the biggest unrecoverable loss.
4. **Say motions aloud in a fixed form**, and repeat them back:
   - "Motion: *the monthly water levy rises from 300 to 500 shillings from March.* Proposed by Mr. X, seconded by Mrs. Y."
   - "All in favour? Against? Abstain? **The motion is carried / lost.**"
   Spoken numbers: say the digits slowly, once as a number and once in words.
5. **Name the owner of every action aloud:** "Action: Mrs. Kamau collects three plumber quotes before the 10th."
6. **Read decisions back at the end** (60 seconds). Those read-backs are the cleanest audio in the meeting and the best evidence lines.
7. **Say your name** the first time you speak (helps map `SPEAKER_xx` to people).
8. **Consent:** tell the room it is being recorded, why, who hears it and when it is deleted (see README privacy section).
